Ready to go live?

Your camera and mic stream straight to the player on your website. Use Chrome, Edge or Firefox, and allow the camera when asked.

Checking your website…

One-time setup: after you press Start, VDO.Ninja shows a viewer link containing audience=. Paste it here:

Then open your Admin → Live & Radio → Live player on your website, paste, and hit Save changes. Or just send the link to Claude and say “connect my studio”.

How it works

  1. Hit Start camera & go live and allow your camera and mic.
  2. In the panel that opens, pick your camera and mic and press Start.
  3. First time only: copy the viewer link it shows you (it contains audience=). Paste it into your Admin under Live & Radio → Live player on your website and hit Save changes.
  4. You're live. Keep this tab open. Closing it or hitting End broadcast ends the show.

Stream ID

Your private publishing key comes from your Admin's Go Live button and is never put on the website. Nobody can broadcast to your site without it.

Good to know

Video goes straight from your computer to each viewer, so every viewer uses some of your upload speed. It's great for a small crowd. For a big audience, go live on YouTube or Twitch from your Admin instead.
